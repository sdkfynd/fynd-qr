Privacy at Fynd QR

Updated 26 October 2026

Fynd QR creates static QR codes in your browser and offers Google sign-in for saved codes, logos, editable dynamic links and scan analytics. This notice explains the information those features use.

Creating and sharing a QR code

Static QR content is generated in your browser. It is sent to the account service when you choose to save it. When you upload a logo while signed in, it is also saved to your account’s logo library. A downloaded QR file contains the content you entered, which can include contact information or a Wi-Fi password. Anyone with that file may be able to read that content.

Google sign-in and your session

Sign-in requests only Google’s basic identity scopes: openid, email and profile. Google and Supabase Auth provide and store your email address, name, Google account identifier and basic profile details, which may include a profile image. Supabase also assigns a user ID. Fynd QR uses verified identity information to sign you in and connect you to your saved workspace. It does not request access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Supabase session credentials are stored in this browser so your session can be restored and refreshed. Sign out on shared computers. You can also review or revoke the app’s connection in your Google Account; revoking Google access does not itself delete saved QR data.

Saved codes and logos

The account service stores saved code names, content fields, design choices, logo images and thumbnails, dynamic destinations, scan addresses, status and creation/update information. Saved content can include any personal information or passwords you enter. Ownership records connect this data to your verified account. Account access checks restrict the management of saved work to its owner.

A dynamic QR’s scan link is public. Anyone who has it can resolve its active destination without signing in. The destination is chosen by the code’s creator and may collect information under its own privacy practices.

Dynamic scan analytics and request limits

When someone opens a dynamic link, the service records the time, an estimated device/browser category, an automated-traffic flag and, when supplied, the referring website’s domain. The current deployment records country as Unknown. Code owners can see scan totals and these breakdowns.

The service processes network-address and browser headers to calculate a code-specific keyed hash for estimated visitors. It uses a separate keyed network-address hash to enforce request limits. Raw IP addresses and full browser identifiers are not stored in the app’s scan analytics. These estimates can be affected by shared networks, privacy tools and bots. Hosting providers may keep their own operational and access logs.

Retention and removal

Detailed scan events are marked to expire after 90 days and removed by service cleanup as requests arrive. All-time scan counts remain with the saved code. Old request-limit buckets are cleaned up after one day.

Accounts, saved codes, logos and ownership records have no automatic expiry. Archiving a code hides it from the library and stops its dynamic link; archiving does not erase its stored data. Contact the project owner to request account or saved-data removal. Your identity may need to be verified before a request is acted on. Removing stored data cannot recall downloaded QR files, copies shared with others or information held by destination websites. Provider logs or backups may follow the provider’s own retention schedules.

Hosting and access

GitHub Pages serves the website. Supabase provides authentication, the database and the account/redirect service; the app’s database is configured in Mumbai. These providers process information needed to run their services. The project operator may access stored information for support, administration or troubleshooting.

The app uses HTTPS and restricts database access through its account service. These controls do not guarantee that a service will always be available or that information can never be exposed. Choose carefully what you save and share.

Contact
